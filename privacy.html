Privacy Policy

Venkatkrishnan Iyer (SafeNet Alerts) · Last updated October 22, 2026

SafeNet Alerts is a personal student project by Venkatkrishnan Iyer. A Raspberry Pi fall-detection prototype sends automated text message alerts to the project owner's own mobile phone. This policy explains what data the project uses.

What we collect

We collect only the mobile phone number you provide to receive alerts, along with message delivery records (such as the time a message was sent and whether it was delivered). These records are handled by our messaging provider, Twilio.

How we use it

We use this information only to send alert messages and to troubleshoot delivery. We do not use it for advertising or marketing.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Data is shared only with Twilio, as needed to deliver the messages.

Opting out and deleting your data

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving alerts. To ask for your information to be deleted, contact us at the address below.
